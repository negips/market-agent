# Rules for the trading game.
1. The player starts with a portfolio which contains a certain amount of cash. This fixed by a parameter which can be programmed.
2. The value of portfolio at the beginning is given by the initial value of cash.
3. The player is allowed buy and sell stocks on the BSE or NSE stock exchanges.
4. Cash is used up whenever stocks are bought and cash is received whenever stocks are sold. Whenever stocks are sold the value of the stocks sold goes into a category called "reserved cash". This has the same value as regular cash except that it cannot be used to buy stocks. After a delay of 2 days, the reserve cash is converted into cash.
5. The number of stocks that can be purchased are limited by the amount of cash that is available in the portfolio.
6. The number of stocks sold/bought can only be whole number counts. There can't be any fractional stocks traded.
7. The total value of the portfolio is equal to the total value of the stocks (which is continuously changing) + the amount of cash + the amount of reserve cash.
8. Purchase/selling decisions can only be made when the stock exchanges are open and, once every 15 minutes or, immediately after a news item has been released.
9. A particular purchased stock can be held for a maximum of 10 days. After 10 days the player will be forced to sell the stock.
10. Stocks bought can not be sold again for 1 day.
11. All transactions involving buying and selling of stocks costs 0.5% of the total transaction value as fees. In case of stock purchase this gets deducted from the available cash. In case of stock sale this gets deducted from the cash received from the sale of the stocks.
12. A particular stock holding can not exceed 15% of the total portfolio value.
13. There is a ceiling in the total number of stocks that can be held in the portfolio. We will indicate that by N_MAX which is a parameter that can be set. For now we will set it to N_MAX=20.
14. Cash held in the portfolio can never exceed 30% of the total portfolio value.
15. The objective of the game is to continuously maximize the total value of the portfolio.
