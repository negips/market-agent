Forecaster v4 · every layer in matrix form, in the order the data flows
One training window holds M consecutive minutes of N stocks. Every layer below is written for one window (the batch dimension just repeats it). Vectors are columns, a bold-free capital letter is a matrix whose rows are tokens, and is a column of n ones, so adds the bias b to every row. The layer list and parameter counts are on the Architecture page (Forecaster_v4 tab).
