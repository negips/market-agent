Company price series from the forecaster cache, and their projection on a low-dimensional Legendre basis
Company series
one company of the forecaster cache (the log-returns the model sees, and the close) through the ops server · line colour = period (train / val) when a run of this bar size is on disk
Legendre projection of the price
the company and date range chosen above, fitted by least squares on P₀ … PN−1 (bar position mapped onto [−1, 1]); computed by scripts/tools/legendre_projection.jl
Residual: price − fit, ₹
RMS error against the number of terms, % of the mean price
Coefficients: RMS contribution of each polynomial, |ck| / √(2k+1), ₹ (log scale)
R² is the share of the series' variance the fit keeps (about 0.2/N of it is lost for a random walk, so it is high for small N). Return variance kept is the harder test: the variance of the fit's bar-to-bar log-returns over the variance of the actual ones. A smooth fit keeps roughly N/(number of bars) of it, which is why a low-dimensional basis follows the trend and discards the moves a forecaster has to predict. Step correlation is the correlation between the two return series.
