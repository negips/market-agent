The rules
Verbatim from TradingGameRules.txt (repo root) — the single source of truth every constant in constants.jl and every check in test_rules.jl traces back to. Rule 9 (the 1-day lock-up) was added after Stage 1 shipped and folded into the simulator and its test suite the same day.
1–2
Start with a configurable amount of cash. Initial portfolio value equals that starting cash exactly.
3
Buy and sell stocks on the NSE or BSE.
4
Sale proceeds go into reserved cash — counts toward portfolio value, but can't fund a purchase — for 2 trading days before becoming spendable cash.
5
Purchases are capped by available (non-reserved) cash.
6
Shares trade in whole-number counts only — no fractional shares.
7
Portfolio value = stock value (continuously changing) + cash + reserved cash.
8
Decisions only while markets are open, at most every 15 minutes — or immediately after a news release.
9
A position is force-sold after 10 trading days, no exceptions.
10
A bought lot can't be voluntarily sold again for 1 trading day.
11
Every buy or sell costs 0.5% of the transaction value in fees — deducted from cash on a buy, from proceeds on a sell.
added12
No single stock's holding may exceed 15% of total portfolio value — enforced when buying, not by force-trimming later drift.
added13
At most N_MAX = 20 distinct stocks may be held at once.
14
Objective: continuously maximize total portfolio value.

Policy architecture
One joint decision per step across every candidate stock and the shared cash pool — not independent per-stock scores. ActorCriticPolicy, built entirely from Flux primitives already used elsewhere in this repo.
Per stock
GRU encoder
120-bar hourly window, weight-shared across all N candidates
→
Fusion
+ news + holding
Concat recency-decayed news signal + per-lot hold-state, back to embed_dim
→
Joint decision
MultiHeadAttention
Every stock attends to every other, plus a macro+portfolio "cash token"
→
Output
actor + critic heads
3 action logits (HOLD/SELL/BUY) + buy-weight per stock, plus V(s)

Game v2 vs the original (v1)
Select with --game-version 2 on train_trading_policy.jl (default 1). The version is carried by EpisodeConfig.rules (a GameRules), so one simulator serves both. A checkpoint is tied to its version — v2 adds a cash token to the network — so --resume / --init-from across versions stops with a clear error.
Aspectv1 (original)v2
Fill priceDecision on bar t fills at bar t+1’s closeSame bar: fills at bar t’s own close — the stand-in for a live instantaneous price. The reward for the decision is the move from bar t to t+1.
Holding limitForced exit after MAX_HOLD_DAYS (14) trading daysNo forced exit. A lot held MAX_HOLD_DAYS_V2 (7)+ days costs --hold-penalty × (share of portfolio value in such lots) per bar. Default 0.0 = off.
Cash ceilingPenalty CASH_CEILING_PENALTY_COEF × max(0, cash/value − MAX_CASH_FRACTION) per barSame formula, coefficient from --cash-penalty, default 0.0 = off (under v1, --cash-penalty overrides the constant).
Cash in the modelOnly two scalars in the portfolio tokenCash is a pseudo-stock: an extra token in the attention set, embedded from [cash/value, reserved/value, cap utilisation = cash/value ÷ MAX_CASH_FRACTION, days spent over the cap ÷ 7]. Cash has a cap fraction (MAX_CASH_FRACTION) mirroring a stock’s (MAX_POSITION_FRACTION) and an “age”, like a lot’s days held. It conditions the stocks and the critic; it has no action of its own — the agent raises cash by selling stocks.
Observation4 portfolio scalars6 portfolio scalars (adds cap utilisation and days-over-cap); a stock’s “remaining hold” fraction counts down from 7 days
UnchangedFees, settlement, 1-day lock-up, rebuy cooldown, holdings cap, per-stock position cap, reward window, PPO, masks-after-argmax
Order inside a v2 step: settle reserved cash → mask and fill the action at the current close → advance the clock → settle again for the new date → mark value → reward minus the penalties. The sequence below describes v1; in v2 steps 6a–6c change as above (fill before the clock moves, no forced-exit step).
Validation: one decision step
A validation run is the same rollout code as a training episode, run once every --eval-every iterations over the held-out date window with greedy=true: each candidate takes its single most-likely action instead of a sampled one, and nothing is learned. Its summed reward is val_return, which alone decides whether policy.bson is checkpointed. Below is exactly what runs, in order. Rule numbers refer to constants.jl.
A · Once per validation episode — train.jl → ppo.jl → env.jl
1
train_policy!train.jl
When iter % eval_every == 0 it calls start_episode! (clears the live tracker, tags the phase val), then collect_rollout(env, policy, val_config; greedy=true, live_cb=…).
2
collect_rolloutppo.jl
Takes a CPU copy of the policy (single-bar forward passes are ~10× slower on GPU), calls reset!, and preallocates one observation tensor per bar for the whole episode (T = end_hour_idx − current_hour_idx decision bars).
3
reset!env.jl
Cash ← initial cash; empties holdings, reserved cash and rebuy cooldowns; finds the first and last hourly bar of the window; loads the validation candidate universe from val_config.
B · Every hourly bar — the loop inside collect_rollout; one pass = one decision
1
assemble_observation!observation.jl
Writes the state at the current bar into the preallocated views: per candidate a 120-bar close window divided by its first valid close (+ the previous trading day’s (H−L)/C range), the news features, the holding state (held flag, unrealised %, remaining-hold fraction of the most urgent lot), 10 days × 9 macro series, and 4 portfolio ratios (cash, reserved, value/initial, stocks). Nothing after this bar is visible.
2
stack_observationsobservation.jl
Adds a batch dimension (B = 1) so the observation matches the network’s input shape.
3
policy(…) — ActorCriticPolicypolicy.jl
Hourly GRU encodes each stock (last timestep kept) → fused with its news + holding features → a “portfolio token” built from the macro encoder and portfolio ratios is appended → one MultiHeadAttention pass over all N+1 tokens, so every stock sees every other and the cash state → actor head gives 3 logits (HOLD/SELL/BUY) and 1 buy-weight logit per stock; critic gives V(s).
4
softmax → argmaxppo.jl
Logits become probabilities (3, N). With greedy=true each candidate independently takes argmax of its three. The buy weight is sigmoid(buy_weight_logit). No rule is applied yet — the policy can choose SELL for a stock it doesn’t hold.
5
RawAction listppo.jl
One RawAction(sym_idx, kind, weight) per candidate is built and handed to step!.
6
step!env.jl
Runs sub-steps 6a–6f below and returns a StepResult(reward, done, info).
6a
_advance_clock!env.jl
Moves to the next hourly bar. Every price used from here on is that bar’s, so a decision made on bar t trades at bar t+1’s price.
6b
_settle_reserved_cash!env.jl
Reserved-cash lots whose settlement date has arrived (sale + 2 trading days, rule 4) move into spendable cash — before anything else, so settled cash is usable this bar.
6c
_force_exit_stale_holdings!env.jl
Any lot held ≥ MAX_HOLD_DAYS is sold unconditionally (rule 9, exempt from the lock-up) via _execute_sell! with kind forced_exit: proceeds minus 0.5% fee go to a new reserved-cash lot, and that symbol’s rebuy cooldown starts. This runs before the policy’s action is applied.
6d
resolve_actionsaction.jl
The only place the rules mask the policy’s choice (see the list below). A masked action silently becomes HOLD; surviving BUYs are sized from the cash pool.
6e
_apply_actions!env.jl
Executes the surviving trades. SELL: every lot of that symbol past the lock-up is sold at the bar’s price (_execute_sell!). BUY: whole shares only, floor(notional / price), debits cash by notional + 0.5% fee and opens a new Holding.
6f
portfolio_breakdown → rewardenv.jl
Value = stocks + cash + reserved. _log_return_reward! pays 0 until REWARD_INTERVAL_DAYS trading days have passed, then the whole window’s log(V / V_window_start) (the last bar flushes a shorter tail). The rule-14 penalty coef × max(0, cash/value − 30%) is subtracted on every bar.
7
_annotate_trade_probs!ppo.jl
Stamps each trade event of this bar with the actor’s p(hold)/p(sell)/p(buy) for that stock — the record of what the policy itself wanted, including at forced exits.
8
RolloutStep + live_cbppo.jl · live.jl
The step is stored in the buffer. The live callback appends the bar to the tracker’s value curve and trade list and rewrites live_status.json every 5 bars.
C · After the last bar — back in train_policy!
1
val_return, val_valuetrain.jl
val_return = sum of every bar’s reward over the episode (telescopes to log(V_final / V_initial) minus the cash penalties); val_value = final portfolio value.
2
save_val_run!live.jl
Appends one line — value curve, every trade, final holdings — to val_runs.jsonl (never overwritten). This is what the live page’s validation charts and trades table read.
3
checkpoint checktrain.jl
If val_return beats the best so far, the policy is saved to policy.bson. Training return never checkpoints when a validation window exists.
What resolve_actions masks — in this order, after the policy has already chosen
SELL
Not held, or every lot still inside the 1-day lock-up (rule 10) → HOLD. Selling closes all eligible lots of that stock.
BUY · universe
Symbol not in this episode’s candidate list → HOLD.
BUY · cooldown
Opening a new position in a stock sold within the last REBUY_COOLDOWN_DAYS (rule 15) → HOLD. Adding to a stock still held is exempt.
BUY · holdings cap
If the portfolio already holds n_max_holdings(N) symbols (rule 13), new positions beyond the free slots are dropped — chosen at random (uses the rollout RNG), not by list order.
BUY · cash
Surviving buys split the cash held at the start of this bar in proportion to their buy weights, net of the fee (rule 5). Same-bar sale proceeds and reserved cash are not spendable.
BUY · size cap
Each buy is capped so that stock’s value is at most MAX_POSITION_FRACTION of the portfolio (rule 12). Cash freed by the cap is left unspent, not redistributed.
Things worth remembering
Timing
The policy sees bar t and trades at bar t+1’s hourly close, not the price it was shown. Channel 2 (daily range) uses the previous trading day, so nothing after the decision bar is visible. (It used to read the current day’s complete range and volume, a look-ahead leak; the volume channel was removed.)
Masks come last
The network is never told which actions are blocked. A high p(sell) with zero executed sells can mean the lock-up or “not held” mask is eating it; compare p_sell in the trades table with what actually happened.
All-at-once buying
Every candidate whose argmax is BUY is bought on the same bar, splitting the whole cash pool. With many BUYs on the first bar the portfolio goes fully invested immediately (each position then capped at 15%).
Forced exit first
The 14-day exit runs before the policy’s action, so a stock can be force-sold and the same bar’s BUY for it is blocked by the fresh cooldown.
Greedy ≠ training
Training samples from the probabilities, so exploration can try SELL even when it is not the argmax. Validation never does — a policy that never ranks SELL first will never sell in validation, whatever training saw.

A policy that had learned nothing would hover near zero. This is a mechanics sanity check on synthetic data, not a real-market backtest.

Quick start
Simulate
using TradingGame, StockSwingPredictor cache = load_inference_cache("website/data/inference_cache.bson") env = TradingGameEnv(cache) reset!(env, EpisodeConfig( initial_cash = 1_000_000.0, start_date = Date(2024, 1, 1), end_date = Date(2024, 6, 30), candidate_universe = ["INFY", "TCS", "RELIANCE"], )) result = step!(env, heuristic_policy(env)) result.reward # log-return of portfolio value this step portfolio_value(env)
Train
policy = ActorCriticPolicy() cfg = EpisodeConfig(initial_cash=1_000_000.0, start_date=Date(2024,1,1), end_date=Date(2024,3,31), candidate_universe=["INFY","TCS","RELIANCE"]) policy, log = train_policy!(policy, env, cfg; iterations=100, checkpoint_path="policy.bson", episode_log_path="episode_log.jsonl", stop_file="STOP") log["train_return"] # should trend upward
